REFERENCE: GEN 4:15
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The WIP says that the killer's punishment will be seven times more severe than Cain's punishment ("هفت برابر شدیدتر از مجازات تو"). The reference instead promises that anyone who kills Cain will suffer vengeance seven times over; it does not establish a comparison with Cain's punishment.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise the statement so that it communicates sevenfold vengeance on Cain's killer without adding a comparison to Cain's punishment.

REFERENCE: GEN 5:24
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The WIP adds that God took Enoch "to his presence upward" ("به حضور خود به بالا برد"). The reference states only that God took him away; the added destination and upward movement are not expressed there.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise to retain God's taking Enoch without adding the destination or manner unless this clarification is supported by authorised translation policy.

REFERENCE: GEN 6:4
TYPE: TERMINOLOGY
ACTION LEVEL: REVIEW
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The WIP replaces the group name "Nephilim" with the interpretive description "مردانی غول‌آسا" (giant men). The reference retains the designation Nephilim and separately calls them heroes of old and men of renown.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Review the terminology and preserve the identifying designation, or provide a non-interpretive rendering that does not equate the Nephilim with giants without stated project authority.
