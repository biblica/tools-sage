REFERENCE: GEN 9:19
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The WIP says that all the peoples of the world came from Noah's three sons ("همۀ اقوام دنیا از سه پسر نوح به وجود آمدند") but omits the reference statement that people were scattered over the whole earth from them.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise to retain both descent from Noah's three sons and their dispersion over the whole earth.

REFERENCE: GEN 9:27
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The WIP renders Japheth's dwelling in Shem's tents as being made a partner in Shem's happiness ("او را شریک سعادت سام گرداند"). This replaces the reference image and assertion, "may Japheth live in the tents of Shem," with a different benefit.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise to express Japheth dwelling in Shem's tents while preserving the surrounding blessing and Canaan's servitude.

REFERENCE: GEN 10:1
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The WIP says Noah's sons themselves were born after the flood ("پسران نوح، که بعد از طوفان متولد شدند"), whereas the reference says that these sons had sons after the flood. The WIP changes the generation and chronology.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise the relative clause so it states that Shem, Ham, and Japheth had sons after the flood.

REFERENCE: GEN 10:9
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The WIP changes Nimrod from a mighty hunter before the Lord to an expert archer and changes the proverb into a blessing about archery ("تیرانداز ماهری"; "خداوند تو را در تیراندازی مانند نمرود گرداند"). This omits both hunting and the comparison's "before the Lord" element.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise to identify Nimrod as a mighty hunter before the Lord and retain the corresponding proverb.

REFERENCE: GEN 10:30
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The WIP says the people were scattered from Mesha to the eastern hills of Sephar ("پراکنده بودند"), while the reference defines the region where they lived as stretching from Mesha toward Sephar. This changes a geographic residence boundary into dispersion.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise to describe the extent of the region where they lived, from Mesha toward Sephar in the eastern hill country.
