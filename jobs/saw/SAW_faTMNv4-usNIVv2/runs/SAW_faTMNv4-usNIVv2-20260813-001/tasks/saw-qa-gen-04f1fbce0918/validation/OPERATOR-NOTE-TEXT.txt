REFERENCE: GEN 1:14
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The WIP renders the lights as signs for «فصل‌ها، روزها و سال‌ها» (seasons, days, and years), whereas the reference includes sacred times. The sacred/appointed observance component is not conveyed.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise the expression for «فصل‌ها» so that the signs also mark the intended sacred or appointed times, while retaining days and years.

REFERENCE: GEN 2:14
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: MEDIUM
ISSUE:
The WIP says the Tigris «به سوی شرق آشور جاری است» (flows toward the east of Assyria), while the reference locates it along the east side of Ashur. The directional wording can change the geographic relation.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise the wording to express that the river runs along or on the east side of Ashur, not toward its east.

REFERENCE: GEN 2:23
TYPE: TERMINOLOGY
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The WIP has Adam call the woman «نسا», followed by a note claiming it means woman. This is not the ordinary Persian term «زن» and makes the naming statement unclear.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE, PROJECT-GRAMMAR

RECOMMENDATION:
Replace or explain the nonstandard term with an understandable Persian rendering for “woman,” preserving the stated relationship to man.
