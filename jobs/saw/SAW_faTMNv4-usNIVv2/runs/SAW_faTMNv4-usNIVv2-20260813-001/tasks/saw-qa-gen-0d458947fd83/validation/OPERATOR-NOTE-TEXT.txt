REFERENCE: GEN 6:13
TYPE: TERMINOLOGY
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The WIP says people filled the earth with «شرارت» (evil/wickedness), whereas the reference specifies that the earth was filled with violence because of them. This removes the repeated violence motif that explains the announced judgment.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise the wording to explicitly retain violence as the condition filling the earth, while preserving the causal connection to the people.

REFERENCE: GEN 7:14
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The WIP lists domestic and wild animals as «بزرگ و کوچک» (large and small), but the reference separately includes every creature moving along the ground and repeatedly specifies each according to its kind. The replacement category does not preserve the stated inclusion of ground-moving creatures.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Restore an explicit category covering creatures that move along the ground and retain the reference’s kind-based grouping without substituting large/small classification.
