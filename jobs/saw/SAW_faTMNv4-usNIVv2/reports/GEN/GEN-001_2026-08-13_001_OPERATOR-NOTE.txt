REFERENCE: GEN 1:7
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The WIP says God made the expanse “to separate” the lower waters from the upper waters ("تا ... جدا کند"), expressing purpose, but does not state that he actually separated them or retain the reference’s completion statement “And it was so.” This weakens the accomplished creation action.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise the verse to state that God separated the waters below the expanse from those above it, preserving the completed result.

REFERENCE: GEN 1:14
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: MEDIUM
ISSUE:
The WIP renders the lights’ calendrical function only as “showing seasons, days, and years” ("نشان دادن فصل‌ها، روزها و سال‌ها"). The authorised reference specifies that the signs mark “sacred times,” a function not conveyed by ordinary “seasons.”

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise the rendering so that the signs’ role in marking sacred/appointed times is explicit, while retaining days and years.

REFERENCE: GEN 1:30
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The WIP says that God gave green plants to the animals ("همۀ علف‌های سبز را ... بخشیدم") but omits that they were given “for food.” The food provision is therefore not explicitly communicated.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Add wording that identifies every green plant as food for the listed animals and every creature with the breath of life.
