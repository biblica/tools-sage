REFERENCE: GEN 1:11
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The WIP calls the trees merely «درختان میوه‌دار» and does not retain the reference qualification that the fruit contains seed. The following clause about producing their own kind does not express fruit with seed in it.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise the command so that fruit trees are explicitly described as bearing fruit containing seed, while retaining the distinction between seed-bearing plants and fruit trees.

WIP, REFERENCE

REFERENCE: GEN 1:21
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The reference describes sea creatures and birds as created according to their kinds. The WIP instead says they «نوع خود را تولید می‌کنند», asserting that they produce their own kinds; this changes classification at creation into a reproductive action.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise the wording to state that the creatures and winged birds were created according to their respective kinds, without substituting a claim about reproduction.

WIP, REFERENCE
