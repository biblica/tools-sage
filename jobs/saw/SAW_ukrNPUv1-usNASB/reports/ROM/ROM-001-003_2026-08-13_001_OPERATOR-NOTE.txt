REFERENCE: ROM 1:5
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
«привести до послуху віри всі народи» makes all nations the direct object to be brought into obedience. The reference describes bringing about the obedience of faith among all the Gentiles, not an assertion that every nation is brought into that obedience.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise the clause so that «послух віри» is brought about among/all the nations, without making «всі народи» the direct object of «привести».

REFERENCE: ROM 1:11
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
«розділити з вами якийсь духовний дар» means to share a spiritual gift with the Romans. The reference states that Paul wants to impart a gift to them for their strengthening; the direction of the gift is lost.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Replace «розділити з вами» with wording that clearly conveys giving or imparting a spiritual gift to the Romans.

REFERENCE: ROM 1:27
TYPE: TERMINOLOGY
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
«належне покарання за свої збочення» renders the stated «error» as «perversions», adding a more specific and evaluative characterization than the reference expresses for the basis of the penalty.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Use a Ukrainian term corresponding to «error»/«wrongdoing» that does not add the specific characterization «збочення».

REFERENCE: ROM 1:29
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The list has «розпусти» where the reference includes «evil». «Розпуста» introduces sexual debauchery, which is not the listed item in the reference, and the reference’s distinct item «evil» is consequently absent.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise the first list so its four items correspond to unrighteousness, wickedness, greed, and evil, without adding «розпуста».

REFERENCE: ROM 2:15
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The WIP says the thoughts "звинувачують, то виправдовують одна одну" (accuse or justify one another). The reference says that the Gentiles' thoughts alternately accuse or defend them. The target changes the object of accusation/defense from the people to the thoughts themselves.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise the clause so that the alternating thoughts accuse or defend the Gentiles/them, while retaining the intended alternation.

REFERENCE: ROM 3:4
TYPE: QUOTATION
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
The quotation is materially recast. "Ти справедливо виконуєш Свій вирок" replaces being justified in God's words, and "бездоганно здійснюєш Твій суд" replaces prevailing when God is judged. This changes both quoted assertions and removes the passive circumstance of God being judged.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Rework the quotation to preserve justification in God's words and prevailing when He is judged, using Ukrainian quotation wording appropriate to project policy.

REFERENCE: ROM 3:9
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
ISSUE:
"Чи є в нас якась перевага?" asks whether Jews have any advantage, whereas the reference asks whether "we" are better than others. In the immediate argument, Jews have already been said to possess an advantage (3:2); the intended question denies moral superiority, not every advantage.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise the question to express whether "we" are better/superior to others, preserving the following denial and the universal charge of sin.

REFERENCE: ROM 3:25
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: MEDIUM
ISSUE:
The WIP attaches "через віру" to God's appointment of Christ and places "в Його крові" with the demonstration of righteousness. The reference presents Christ as the propitiation in His blood through faith. This restructuring obscures or changes the stated relation of Christ's blood and faith to the propitiation.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Revise the sentence so that Christ's blood and faith are clearly connected to the means of propitiation/reconciliation, while preserving the subsequent purpose of demonstrating God's righteousness.

REFERENCE: ROM 3:28
TYPE: MEANING
ACTION LEVEL: CHANGE
CONFIDENCE: MEDIUM
ISSUE:
"Людина може бути виправдана вірою" can express mere possibility ('can be justified'), while the reference is Paul's declarative conclusion that a person is justified by faith apart from works of the Law.

EVIDENCE:
WIP, REFERENCE

RECOMMENDATION:
Use an unambiguously declarative Ukrainian formulation that states justification by faith apart from works of the Law rather than only its possibility.
