# INSPECT contract

## Purpose

Inspect the exact bounded SOURCE scope using the SOURCE as the exclusive content authority, the routed SOURCE-language grammar contract, the routed DONOR lexical packet, and applicable local semantic evidence. INSPECT identifies translation challenges and reusable evidence without drafting TARGET Scripture. Original-language Scripture is not routed during INSPECT.

## Required outputs

- SOURCE lexical entries, forms, contextual senses, ambiguity, and discourse observations supported by routed evidence.
- Exact Scripture references inside the task scope.
- Translation challenges and concise guidance for the later governed BIC stages.
- Proposed memory changes or conflicts only when they are reusable and suitable for explicit human review.

## Limits

- Do not produce TARGET Scripture.
- Do not expect, request, or reconstruct Greek/Hebrew Scripture during INSPECT.
- Do not reconstruct DONOR verse wording, sequence, syntax, propositions, or discourse from the lexical packet.
- Do not use pre-existing TARGET Scripture as evidence.
- Do not import content from unrouted references.
- Do not silently overwrite approved memory.
- Keep occurrence applicability separate from reusable sense records.
